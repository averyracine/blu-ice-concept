What I'd fix

  1. Some visitors are hitting a dead page

    bluicemedspa.com without the "www" throws a security error. Only the www version loads. Your old Fargo link, which directories still point to, lands on a 404.

    Fix: point both addresses to the same site and redirect the old links.

  2. Only Woodbury is on the site

    Chanhassen and Fargo aren't mentioned anywhere. Someone in Fargo has no way to know you're there.

    Fix: a page for each location with its own address, map, hours and booking.

  3. Leftover template text is live

    The contact page shows "Replace with your Google Maps embed code" where the map should be.

    Fix: real maps, and a full pass to clear out the filler.

  4. The reviews read like filler

    First name, last initial, and a flat 5.0 with no link. Meanwhile you have over a hundred real reviews on Google that the site isn't using.

    Fix: pull your real Google reviews in live.

  5. "Book Consultation" doesn't book anything

    It opens a contact form that says you'll reply within 24 hours. People go with whoever lets them pick a time right now.

    Fix: real online scheduling. Pick a location, pick a time, done.

  6. Google can't tell your pages apart

    Every page has the same title, "Blu Ice Medspa: Woodbury, MN", and there's no sitemap. That makes it hard to rank for anything outside Woodbury.

    Fix: a proper title and description per page, a sitemap, and business listings data for each location.

  7. The team section is thin

    First names only, and no medical director listed. For a medical practice, that's the first thing a careful patient looks for.

    Fix: full names, credentials and your medical director, front and center. Your team photos are great, so I made them bigger.

What I'd do with the CRM

Right now the only ways to reach you from the site are a contact form and a Gmail address. That means somebody on your team has to see it, remember it, and chase it. Here's how I'd set it up so the system does the chasing.

1 · CaptureEvery form, call, text and DM lands in one place, tagged by location and treatment.
2 · RespondThe lead gets a text back within a minute, even after hours.
3 · Follow upReminders, no-show rebooking and consult follow-ups go out on their own.
4 · TrackYou see which ad or post each booking came from.
  • Less front desk time. Your team stops digging through inboxes and returning calls a day late.
  • Nothing slips. Every lead has an owner, a status and a next step.
  • More reviews. A review request goes out after each visit.
  • Old patients come back. Past patients get a reason to rebook.
  • One view per location. You can see Woodbury, Chanhassen and Fargo side by side.
  • You know what's working. Spend goes to what's bringing in booked consults.
Why I'm bringing this up now
